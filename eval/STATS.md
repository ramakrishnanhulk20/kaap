## How the labels were made

The labels are AI-made. Each one was written by an AI labeller from the guide and the raw proposal, without any Kaap code, and `node scratchpad/eval-e12c/check.mjs` re-derives every rule from the raw kind and context. They were cross-checked blind: a second AI labeller that never saw the answer key labelled 45 records (30 real, 15 planted) on its own, and labelled four of them again on 26 Oct 2026 (fast-auth-maintainer #4, jhptc #3, sweat-multisig #0, sweat-multisig #4) with the policy before read from the chain.
