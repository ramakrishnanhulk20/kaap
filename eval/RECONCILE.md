## 26 Oct 2026: chain re-read of the policy before, guide v2.3 and Ram's rule A

### The chain re-read of the policy before
Before 26 Oct the policy before a real policy proposal was rebuilt by replaying approved proposals in id order, and 25 of the 73 real policy-kind records had it unknown. On 26 Oct (eval-e12a) every one of the 73 was read from an archival node at the moment its status names (guide v2.3 section 7; the method is written out in `eval/real/SAMPLING.md`). The node answered for all 73, so `before_unknown` is on none: 25 records gained a policy before, 4 open proposals moved to the policy at the snapshot block, and 44 were already right. Id order is wrong where proposals executed out of it; guide section 7 gives an example where a later full ChangePolicy undid a member added in between.

### Guide v2.3 and Ram's rule A
Guide v2.3 (`docs/eval/labeling-guide.md`) changes three things. Section 7 names the moment of the policy before per status and orders proposals by execution, not id. The section 4 DESC_CONTRADICTS row counts removing a member from a role that can approve, and any change that fires GUARD_WEAKENED, as a change of control, so the finding is critical on them (ruling 23). Rulings 22 to 27 settle the cases the re-read raised.
Ram's rule A (26 Oct 2026, ruling 22): for a policy proposal whose description names specific member, role or threshold changes, DESC_CONTRADICTS also fires when, measured against the policy before, the effects make a control change the description does not mention (a member added to or removed from a role that can approve, or fewer votes needed), or when a described change is already in place.
The labels were redone in two layers by eval-e12b: 29 real relabels for the records whose policy before changed, then 4 real v2.3 finals and 2 planted finals (#48 and #49, from `final-v23-planted-alt.jsonl`, the reading ruled in). `scratchpad/eval-e12c/build.mjs` merges them, keyed by set and id, and stamps guide_version v2.3 on every record (it was v2.2).
29 Oct 2026: guide section 3 gave every members_added and members_removed entry an `approver` field (true when the role can approve at least one kind in the policy the member is in). `scratchpad/eval-e12d/approver.mjs` sets it from each record's own kind and policy before during the build, `check.mjs` re-derives it with its own test, and no floor, finding or veto field changed on any record (`node scratchpad/eval-e12d/nochange.mjs`).
